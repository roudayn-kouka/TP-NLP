# TALN – Travaux Pratiques

This repository contains practical assignments (TPs) for **Traitement Automatique du Langage Naturel (TALN)**, completed as part of the IDS 4 / IGL 5 programs at the Faculty of Sciences of Tunis (FST), University of Tunis El Manar, during the 2026–2027 academic year.

## 📚 Practical Assignments

- **TP 1 – Text Preprocessing**
  - Text cleaning and normalization
  - Tokenization, stopword removal, stemming, and lemmatization
  - Spelling correction and WordNet
  - Word frequency analysis and collocations

- **TP 2 – Text Preprocessing and Word Vectorization**
  - Bag of Words (BoW) and n-grams
  - FastText embeddings
  - Word2Vec: CBOW and Skip-Gram
  - Cosine similarity and word similarity analysis

- **TP 3 – Speech and Image-to-Text Conversion**
  - Speech recognition and audio transcription
  - Microphone-based speech-to-text
  - Multilingual Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
  - OCR evaluation and grammatical correction

- **TP 7 – Transformers: Encoder**
  - Tokenization and token embeddings
  - Positional encoding
  - Scaled dot-product attention and multi-head attention
  - Layer normalization
  - Feed-forward networks

## 🛠️ Technologies

Python · NLTK · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Gensim · PyTorch · Hugging Face Transformers · Tesseract OCR · EasyOCR · TensorFlow
